## Illustrative change record

For a SaaS access-policy change, I would record:

1. The business purpose, affected identities and data, accountable owner, and required authority.
2. Current configuration and a recovery path that does not depend on the identity being changed.
3. A limited test group, expected allowed and denied behavior, and rollback conditions.
4. Validation findings, exceptions, communications, and handoff instructions.

This is a proposed operating format, not a completed change ticket or restoration test. Production actions require the organization's authority and controls.

## Recovery review

An available backup is different from an accepted restore. A useful recovery record identifies the protected system, backup scope, access owner, recovery dependency, test environment, observed restoration result, and remaining gaps. Recovery time and recovery point targets should be agreed and then measured; no achieved targets are claimed here.
